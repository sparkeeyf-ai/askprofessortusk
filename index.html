ASK PROFESSOR TUSK

Every memory has a lesson.

Ask Professor Tusk is a short animated series. In every episode, a curious young Monkey asks a question, and Professor Tusk, an elephant who remembers everything, tells the true story behind it: who decided it, why, and what it means for you.

New short lessons on history, money, marketing and everyday life are published on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.
