Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy covers the Ask Professor Tusk channels and the "Ask Professor Tusk Publisher" app. The app is a private tool used only by the channel owner to publish the channel's own videos.

What the app does

The Publisher app signs in to the channel owner's own YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts, with the owner's permission. It uses that access to:

  • upload and schedule the channel's videos;
  • set video titles, descriptions and thumbnails;
  • read the channel's own performance statistics, such as views and engagement;
  • read and reply to comments on the channel's own posts.

Data we collect

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data about viewers or other users. Access tokens for the owner's own accounts are stored only on the owner's computer. They are used only for the purposes above.

Comments and public profile names that viewers post on our videos are processed only inside YouTube, Instagram or Facebook, in order to read and reply to them. We do not export or keep them anywhere else.

Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API data is handled under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or from Meta's "Apps and websites" settings. Once access is revoked, the app stops working straight away.
